# Security Policy

AwareBC handles riders' locations, chats and safety alerts, so security problems are taken seriously.

## Supported versions

Only the latest version is supported: the live website and the newest Android app. Updates reach everyone automatically.

## Reporting a problem

**Please don't open a public issue for security problems.**

Email **awarebc.help@gmail.com** with the subject `SECURITY` and include:

- What the problem is and where (website, Android app or server)
- Steps to show it happening
- What someone could do with it
- Your name or handle, if you'd like credit

## What to expect

- A reply within **3 days** confirming we got it
- A fix plan within **14 days** for serious problems
- A note when it's fixed, and credit if you want it

## Please don't

- Access, change or delete other riders' data
- Test on real riders' accounts, or share their locations or chats
- Run attacks that slow down or break the service (no flooding or spam)
- Share the problem publicly before it's fixed

Good-faith research that follows these rules is welcome, and we won't take action against you for it.

Privacy, Terms & Rider Rules

AwareBC by Josh Singer · Updated November 16, 2026

PrivacyRider rulesTermsContact
Short version: no ads, no tracking, no selling your data. Your settings stay on your phone. What you choose to share goes to our small server so other riders can see it, and is deleted on a schedule. Chats are locked so only the people in them can read them. AwareBC is not an emergency service. In an emergency, call 911.

On your phone only

Settings, saved maps and routes, favourites, and your secret rider key. The server keeps only a one-way fingerprint of your key, which gives you your rider tag (like Boss #4K7Q). Back up your key in Friends ▸ Safety & account. Anyone with your key can act as you, so never share it.

What goes to our server, and for how long

  • Login (optional): your rider name, a salted check made on your phone from your password, and your rider key locked with that password. Your password never leaves your phone, so we can’t see or reset it. Deleted with your account.
  • Crash reports: when something breaks, the app sends the error, the app version and your phone type (for example "Android 13, 4 GB"). No name, rider ID or location. Kept 30 days. Turn it off in Settings ▸ Account ▸ Send crash reports.
  • Trail reports and Rig Link posts (spot, note, photo, voice clip, name, tag): public to riders. Photos are shrunk and stripped of camera data first. Delete your own any time.
  • Rider profile (rig details, skills, badges, cover and profile photo): visible to riders who tap you.
  • Friends: your name, tag, friends, requests, groups and who you blocked.
  • Chat: locked on your phone before sending (end-to-end). The server stores only locked text it can't open. Deleted after 30 days; you can unsend.
  • Share my spot: only while it's on, and only to friends you accepted. Erased when sharing stops; never shown if older than 6 hours.
  • Nearby riders (Off-road map): riders within about 60 km see your rig at about 1 km, or exact if you pick Exact. Removed about 15 minutes after you stop. "Show other riders on my map" only changes what you see.
  • Back by: your return time, note, chosen friends and last spot, so friends can be told if you're overdue, even if your phone dies. Deleted 1 day after you check in.
  • Alerts when the app is closed: your phone's push address. Alerts only say who wrote, never what.
  • Google sign-in (optional): only a fingerprint of your Google account ID, never your email or name.

Location and microphone

Location centres the map, guides you home and warns about trouble ahead. It leaves your phone only for the items above. The microphone is used only while you tap the mic for Aware; your phone turns speech into text and AwareBC keeps no audio.

Safety, moderation and deleting your data

Anti-spam counters use a fingerprint of your network address, not the address. Reports of riders or messages are kept 90 days for review; three reports in 30 days pauses that rider's chat for 7 days. Friends ▸ Safety & account ▸ Delete my friends & chat data removes your friends, chats, posts, profile, Back by trips and alert address right away. Riders inactive for a year are deleted automatically.

Services the app uses

Our servers on Cloudflare; maps and places from Esri, OpenStreetMap, OpenFreeMap and Photon; BC data from DataBC, DriveBC and BC Wildfire; weather from Environment Canada, Open-Meteo, NOAA and RainViewer; flights from adsb.lol, FlyItalyADSB (flyitalyadsb.com), licensed CC BY-SA 4.0, and adsbdb. They see the area being looked up and your IP address, under their own privacy policies.

Children

For riders 13 and older. Under 18: only add people you know in real life, with a parent's OK.

Rider rules

  • Delete my account: Settings ▸ Account ▸ Delete my account removes your profile, rig, badges, photos, friends, chats, posts and places from our server, and clears the app on your phone. Riders who stop using the app are removed automatically.
  • Only add riders you know or trust. Never share your rider key.
  • No harassment, threats, hate, sexual content, scams, spam or selling.
  • Don't post someone's location or personal details without their OK.
  • Be honest in trail reports. No fake hazards, and don't hide real ones.
  • Don't pretend to be someone else, AwareBC staff or a moderator.
  • Use Block and Report. Blocked riders can't add you, message you or see your spot, and aren't told. Manage them in Settings ▸ Blocked riders.

Terms

  • Not an emergency service. 🆘 and Back by alert your friends; they don't call 911 or search and rescue, and friends may not see them in time. In an emergency call 911, or use a satellite SOS device with no signal.
  • Reports, roads, weather, fire, snow and flight info come from riders and outside sources and can be wrong, late or missing. Use your own judgement and follow signs.
  • AwareBC is free and provided "as is", without warranty. Features may change or stop.
  • You're responsible for what you post. We may remove content or pause riders who break the rules.

Contact

Questions, safety concerns or data requests: email awarebc.help@gmail.com. To delete your account, use Settings ▸ Account ▸ Delete my account, or see Support.

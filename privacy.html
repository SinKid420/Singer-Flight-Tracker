Sky Overhead – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Sky Overhead is a free live flight tracker. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and we do not sell or share personal data.

What stays on your device

Your settings, your optional API key and cached flight routes are stored only in your device's local storage. They are never sent to us.

Location

If you tap the locate button, the app asks for your device location to centre the map and to show nearby planes and local weather. It is used only in the app and is not stored on any server of ours.

Third-party services the app contacts

  • Flight positions: adsb.lol (through a small relay we run on Cloudflare Workers) and FlyItalyADSB. These receive the map centre coordinates and search radius you are viewing, plus your IP address as part of any web request.
  • Flight routes: adsbdb.com receives the flight callsign of a plane you tap.
  • Weather and radar: Open-Meteo, Environment and Climate Change Canada, NOAA/Iowa Environmental Mesonet and RainViewer receive coordinates and map tile requests.
  • Map tiles and photos: OpenStreetMap and other tile providers, and Planespotters.net for aircraft photos.

The relay does not keep logs of requests beyond what Cloudflare provides for operating the service. Each third party has its own privacy policy.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

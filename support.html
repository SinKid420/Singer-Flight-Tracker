Quick fixes

No planes or data? Settings ▸ App updates ▸ Check data sources.
Location off? Tap GPS check on the map card, then allow location for AwareBC.
Old version? Settings ▸ App updates ▸ Check for updates.

Delete your account and data

In the app: Settings ▸ Account ▸ Delete my account, then type DELETE. It’s removed right away.
No app? Email your rider tag (like Sam #4K7Q) and “Delete my account”. Done within 7 days.
